Franchise Mode Guide

Franchise Mode lets players take control of an NFL team and build it over multiple seasons. You can rebuild a team, draft rookies, make trades, develop young players, manage contracts, and try to create a championship dynasty.

Best Rebuild Teams

Good rebuild teams are usually teams with young players, draft picks, or weak rosters that need improvement. Teams like the Cardinals, Panthers, Patriots, Giants, and Titans can be fun rebuild choices.

Best Win-Now Teams

Win-now teams already have strong rosters and are better for players who want to compete right away. Teams like the Chiefs, Eagles, 49ers, Ravens, Bills, and Cowboys are strong choices.

Draft Strategy

The draft is one of the best ways to build a team. Focus on important positions like quarterback, offensive line, cornerback, pass rusher, and wide receiver.

Player Development

Young players can improve over time if they get playing time and training focus. Developing players is important because it saves money and helps your team improve long-term.

How to Start a Rebuild

  • Choose a team that has weaknesses but also has future potential.
  • Trade older players if they have high value but do not fit your future plan.
  • Collect draft picks when possible.
  • Build around young players with good speed, strength, or development traits.
  • Do not spend all your salary cap too early.

Drafting Tips

Scout Important Positions First

Quarterback, offensive line, cornerback, edge rusher, and wide receiver are some of the most important positions to scout because they can change your whole team.

Look for Athletic Traits

Speed, acceleration, strength, agility, and change of direction can make a player more useful, especially for receivers, corners, running backs, and linebackers.

Do Not Only Draft by Overall

A player with a lower overall can still become great if they have good physical ratings or a strong development trait.

Build Through the Trenches

Offensive line and defensive line are not always the flashiest picks, but they help your team win by protecting the quarterback and pressuring the opponent.

Smart Trades

Trade for young players or draft picks instead of overpaying for older stars. Good trades should help your team now and later.

Contract Management

Do not give huge contracts to every player. Save money for your best players and important positions.

Free Agency

Free agency is good for filling holes, but it can get expensive. Use it to add key players, not to build your entire team.

Depth Matters

A good Franchise team needs backups too. Injuries and fatigue can hurt your team if you only focus on starters.

Weekly Strategy Tips

  • Train players who are young and have high potential.
  • Use weekly strategy to focus on stopping the other team’s biggest strength.
  • If the opponent has a strong quarterback, focus on pass coverage or pass rush.
  • If the opponent has a strong running back, focus on stopping the run.
  • Upgrade players often so their skill points do not sit unused.

Franchise Mistakes to Avoid

  • Trading away all your draft picks too early.
  • Overpaying old players who will regress soon.
  • Ignoring offensive line and defense.
  • Only drafting skill positions like receiver and running back.
  • Not checking salary cap before signing players.
  • Keeping players who do not fit your team’s future.

Best Long-Term Franchise Plan

The best way to build a strong Franchise team is to create a balance between young talent, smart contracts, and good drafting. A good team should have a franchise quarterback, a solid offensive line, fast defensive players, and enough salary cap space to keep its stars. If you draft well and avoid bad contracts, your team can stay competitive for many seasons.
