Offensive Schemes

Spread Offense: Good for open space, quick passes, deep routes, and mismatches.

West Coast: Good for short passes, safe throws, slants, drags, and consistent drives.

Run Heavy: Good for controlling the clock and forcing the defense to respect the run.

Defensive Schemes

Nickel Zone: Good for stopping deep passes and covering multiple receivers.

Man Blitz: Good for pressure, but risky if your corners cannot cover.

Run Stop: Good for short yardage and goal line defense.

Useful Play Situations

  • One-play touchdown: Look for deep crossers, streak routes, wheel routes, and play action shots.
  • Third down conversion: Use mesh, slants, corner routes, and safe checkdowns.
  • Red zone offense: Use quick passes, inside runs, and routes that attack the sidelines.
  • Stop deep passes: Use Cover 3, Cover 4, and keep safety help over the top.
  • Stop the run: Use heavier fronts, pinch the defensive line, and user-control a linebacker.
